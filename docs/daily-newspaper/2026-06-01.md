### Most important — expanded

**1. MuSig2 nonce leak in remote-signing — pattern to check in fulmine**

On 2026-05-29 the ts-sdk and dotnet-sdk landed "watch-only + remote-signing" as a signer-provider capability (Andrew Camilleri / Pietro Grandi). Within hours of that merge, a same-day security fix shipped: `fix(wallets): keep MuSig2 secret nonce inside the signer` — the private nonce had been crossing the `IRemoteSignerTransport` boundary, which is a textbook nonce-reuse-into-private-key-recovery vector for MuSig2. The fix kept the nonce inside the signer process and only passed the partial signature across the transport. **Why this matters for you:** fulmine has its own signer surface for VHTLC/Boltz flows, and if remote signing is on your roadmap, this is the bug to *not* re-introduce. The arkana logs flag deployment-status as the open question — confirm with @U07ML5CM486 whether the phase-1 remote-signing was ever live in production before the patch.

**3. rust-sdk v0.9.1 TLS regression — quick sanity check on go-sdk equivalents**

If your `ClientType` selection has both an `auto` and a `manual` dial flow, that's the surface to check.
