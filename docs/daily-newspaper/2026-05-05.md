### Most important — expanded

**1. boltz-swap v0.3.26 — 10-strike 404 safety net + v0.3.25 release (Pietro Grandi)**
What: `2893b2a` adds typed 404 detection in `boltz-swap-provider.ts` (`src/errors.ts` for the typed error), and `swap-manager.ts` tracks a per-swap consecutive-unknown counter; after 10 consecutive 404s the swap stops being polled and is marked failed (`a3c4a0f` notifies subscribers when the safety-net trips). v0.3.25 (`acddfb7`) and v0.3.26 (`0ada949`) both shipped today, after `8f65701` upgrades the package to ts-sdk v0.4.23.
Who: Pietro Grandi, merged today (2026-05-04 14:44 → 17:02 +0200, PR #138).
Why for Dusan: This is the **second sibling SDK** in two days to land the same pattern (after dotnet-sdk #80 yesterday). The design is now derisked — bounded retry counter + typed `BoltzSwapNotFoundException` + metadata/event hint. go-sdk's Boltz integration still has the forever-Pending failure mode, and yesterday's TL;DR ("Mirror dotnet-sdk #80") just got upgraded from "one sibling did this" to "two siblings did this and arkade-wdk is already on the new boltz-swap". Mirroring in go-sdk closes a parity gap that's now visibly widening.

**2. ts-sdk v0.4.23 — bigint asset amounts (Andrew Camilleri / kukks, #472, `36fa1b5`)**
What: Breaking change. `Asset.amount` and `AssetDetails.supply` flip from `number` to `bigint` across the entire ts-sdk asset surface — providers (`indexer`, `expoIndexer`), `utils/transactionHistory` (Map<string, bigint>), `wallet.getBalance`, `wallet-message-handler.GET_BALANCE`, `wallet.send` change-output accounting, `delegator.makeSignedDelegateIntent`, `wallet/asset.ts` greedy selection, `validation.ts`. A new `sumAssetAmounts(assets, assetId?)` helper is exported from the package root to soften the migration. Issuance/Reissuance/BurnParams also bigint-ized. Tests mechanically converted (`amount: N` → `amount: Nn`).
Who: kukks, merged Mon May 4 10:55 +0200 as `36fa1b5`, then released as v0.4.23 (`d0ee956`, Pietro).
Why for Dusan: go-sdk doesn't have to follow blindly — Go's `int64` is fine for asset amounts up to ~9.2 × 10^18 — but if go-sdk mirrors ts-sdk types or shares fixtures, decide the parity stance *now* before downstream PRs (fulmine, wallet via go-sdk) start importing the new types. Two paths: (a) hold the line on `int64` and document the divergence, or (b) move to `*big.Int` to match ts-sdk semantics and avoid silent overflow on hostile asset issuers. arkade-wdk picked up v0.4.23 today; the wire format hasn't changed, only the typing.

**3. ts-sdk #468 — export anchor helpers (James, `d25866e`)**
What: Adds nine lines to `src/index.ts` exporting `TxWeightEstimator` (fee calc), `VSize` type, `timelockToSequence` (RelativeTimelock → bip68 sequence), `sequenceToTimelock` (bip68 sequence → RelativeTimelock).
Who: James (gringokiwi), merged Mon May 4 06:09 -0300.
Why for Dusan: These are exactly the helpers go-sdk callers (fulmine, wallet via go-sdk) end up reimplementing. Pulling Go equivalents up into go-sdk's exported surface — `pkg/client/`-level `TxWeightEstimator` and the timelock/sequence converters — is a small parity item that pays off the moment any caller wants accurate fee estimation or BIP-68 sequence math without an internal import.
