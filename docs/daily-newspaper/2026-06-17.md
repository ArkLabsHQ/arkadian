### Most important — expanded
- **Version/digest headers are live but soft — and one client lies about its version.** arkd v0.9.9 shipped to prod with version and digest headers in soft-launch mode (not required). The blocker to flipping them on is the BTCPay/dotnet plugin, which hardcoded `TargetBuild = "0.9.7"` and now reports the wrong version to prod. For dusan this matters because go-sdk needs the `X-SDK-VERSION`/build-version header surface (arkd #1113/#1114) wired before headers become mandatory, or go-sdk clients risk rejection later.
- **Signer rotation is essentially done everywhere except rust-sdk — and go-sdk is now consolidating it.** The deprecated-signer migration merged across arkd, ts-sdk, dotnet-sdk and go-sdk; today's local go-sdk commits fold the migration into a single output and revert the seed-restore discovery path. arkd #1097 adds a deprecation field to the signer proto, so go-sdk's signer wrap should track it. The open external thread (HD-address cycling unconfirmed for go-sdk, plus "why did we rotate" questions) is squarely in dusan's maintainer lane.
- **introspector changed asset-introspection semantics under v0.0.3.** The canonical-asset-id refactor (#97) rewrote `asset_opcodes.go` and altered `contract_id` behavior, and a new `OP_KECCAK256` opcode landed. Any Arkade Script or contract relying on asset opcodes or contract IDs should be re-validated against v0.0.3 before relying on the old behavior.
