### Most important — expanded

1. **enclave #113 is the most consequential new item ecosystem-wide.** aruokhai opened a 6-file, +207/-65 PR that adds protocol switching and CORS handling to the enclave reverse proxy. Code-review immediately flagged two critical issues: (a) `prevent_destroy=false` on the PCR0 KMS signing key — that's the attestation root of trust and accidental destruction would brick the enclave's identity; (b) `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *` is applied to all `/v1/*` admin endpoints (migration, storage, secrets) instead of being scoped to read-only attestation.
